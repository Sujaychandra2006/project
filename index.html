RESPONSIBLE CAMPUS

Smart Waste Management

We follow the principles of Reduce, Reuse and Recycle to keep our campus clean and environmentally friendly.

01

Reduce

Reduce unnecessary consumption and avoid single-use plastic wherever possible.

02

Reuse

Reuse materials and products instead of throwing them away after one use.

03

Recycle

Separate recyclable materials and send them for proper recycling and processing.

04

Compost

Organic waste can be converted into useful compost for campus gardens.
